📚 Match History

Saving a match here archives final scores, player stats, and the full point-by-point replay permanently, separate from the live game state. It does not clear the current scoreboard — use "Start New Match" in Settings for that (it will offer to archive first, then let you pick best-of-3 or best-of-5).
